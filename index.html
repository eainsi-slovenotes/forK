Somewhere along the way...
I realized---I loved her.

I could talk about just how her beauty makes my heart race. Her smile---takes my breath away without her even knowing. But that's not all there is to her. No, not at all. She is much more than her appearance. Her quiet is physical silence, but I know very well that that beautiful, lively mind of hers is already thinking. Thinking of what she'll do later, thinking of what she'll say next, thinking of---anything really. Anything but me. And maybe I won't be able to tell her this. Not in person. Maybe not ever. But I love her. Watching her from this distance keeps me content. Which is a lie I tell myself all the time. I know it's untrue, that I want to be much more than just a bystander. But for a coward like myself, have I any choice at all?


I heard about a few things from my parents the other day. They said I am to exchange schools next year. I wouldn't mind that normally, but I am not the kind of person that I was anymore. I have someone I really do care about now in ways that matter more than most. And I wouldn't be able to go a day without seeing that person. That beautiful, stubborn girl with her stupid little smile that lights up my day, her frown that turns my skies cloudy, and her frustration that clouds my judgement. It's not fair that one girl could have such power over my very being, but she does. And I'm afraid to stray from such brilliance. I cannot entertain the idea of going to school every day to not even get a glance of her back. I cannot have that, I will not accept it. But the universe has an odd way of pacing its own fate. The characters do not have a choice in the author's story but to follow the script. So if fate charts its way then so be it. If I am forced onto a path where your glasses no longer reflect the fire in my heart, then I will go. Only to pave my way back to you. Every single time.


And if I said I wanted her all to myself, would I be selfish? Would I be unreasonable? With how long I've kept these feelings tucked into the back of my mind... I don't know if I'll be able to hide them when I get to 'goodbye'. Actually, I don't think I'll ever be ready for it. She's been a big part of my daily life, with or without her notice. In fact, the first thought on my mind when I wake up, when I do my bed, when I brush my teeth, when I have breakfast---I wonder if she's doing the same. I wonder if she ever thinks about me like I do her every waking moment. It's odd, to care for someone so much to this point. It's almost scary. But it's also a feeling that keeps me going. That reminds me of my very determination. 'Determination' not just to tell her about my intense feelings but also how much she genuinely means to me. As a person, and as a source of motivation. She makes me lose focus and want to keep going at the same time. It's infuriating. But I love it. And I hope in some part of her soul, her heart, her very being, she would care maybe just a speck of how much I do her.
